Naija Skies

Regional captain, ATR 72-class turboprop. Real Nigerian airports, real runway headings, Nigerian weather.

  • Fly: ↓ pull up, ↑ push down, ←→ bank. W/S throttle. A/D rudder.
  • Aircraft: Z/X flaps, G gear, Space brakes, C or 1–5 cameras, N swap aircraft while parked, M map, F1 guide.
  • Cruise: H autopilot (flies to the approach fix). T speeds up time with autopilot on. P pause.
  • Takeoff: flaps 15, full throttle, rotate near 110 kt. Land with flaps 30 and gear down.
